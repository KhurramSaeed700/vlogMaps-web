# Coarse World Geography

`world-land.json` is Natural Earth's 1:110m land dataset, downloaded from
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson.

Natural Earth map data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.
Bundled locally so coarse geography does not depend on the detailed tile provider.
